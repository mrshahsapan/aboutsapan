About

I’m a Surat-based founder and investor with a hands-on interest in how businesses are built and run. My experience spans trading, fashion, hospitality and business partnerships.

My roles include founding Navkar Trade Hub, building TheSizelly, co-owning The House of Makeba’s Surat branch and serving as a designated partner at Thom Ventures LLP.
